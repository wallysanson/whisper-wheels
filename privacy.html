Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: if you place an order, we receive your name, email, shipping address and order details so we can deliver it; your payment is handled by Whop and we never see your card details. If you join our email list, we save your email and some details about your visit, such as your device, rough location and how you found us. We use this to fulfill orders, send updates and understand which posts and ads are working. We don't sell your information. Email contact@whisperwheels.shop anytime to have it deleted.

Who we are

Whisper Wheels ("we", "us") runs whisperwheels.shop, the online store for a noise-reduction kit for residential trash bins. You can reach us at contact@whisperwheels.shop.

What we collect when you place an order

  • Order details: your name, email address, shipping address, the number of kits you ordered, the amount paid and the date.
  • Payment: payments are processed by Whop. You enter your card or other payment details directly into Whop's secure checkout, and we never see or store them. Whop's own privacy policy covers what it collects.
  • How you found us: campaign tags in the link you used (such as utm_source), any ad click identifier, and the referrer and campaign tags from your first visit, attached to your order so we can see which posts and ads lead to sales.

What we collect when you join the email list

  • Your email address, and the date and time you signed up.
  • Which signup form you used on the page (top or bottom).
  • Approximate location: country, state or region, and city. This is estimated by our hosting provider from your internet connection. We don't use GPS or ask for your precise location.
  • Device and browser details: device type (phone, tablet or computer), operating system, browser, whether you opened the page inside an app such as Instagram or TikTok, your browser's user-agent text, screen and window size, language setting and time zone.
  • How you found us: the website or app that sent you to us (the "referrer"), any campaign tags in the link you clicked (UTM tags such as utm_source or utm_campaign), and any ad click identifier added by an ad platform (such as fbclid from Meta, ttclid from TikTok, gclid from Google or msclkid from Microsoft).
  • Your visit: the page address you landed on, how many times you've visited from that device, how long you were on the page before signing up, and how far down the page you scrolled.
  • Your first visit: when you first visited from that device, which page and link you first arrived through, and the referrer and campaign tags from that first visit. This lets us see the post or ad that first introduced you to us, even if you sign up on a later visit.

Information stored on your device

To remember your first visit and count repeat visits, the site saves a small amount of information in your browser's local storage on your device. That includes the date of your first visit, the page and link you arrived through, the referrer, any campaign tags, and a visit counter. It stays on your device, and is sent to us only if you join the list or place an order. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The checkout is provided by Whop and may set its own cookies needed to process your payment securely.

Advertising and the Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). When you visit our pages, it lets Meta know which pages you viewed and whether you started checkout or placed an order, along with information Meta collects from your browser such as your device, IP address and Meta cookies. We use it to measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. Meta's use of this information is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or limit tracking with your browser's privacy settings.

Information we use briefly and don't keep

We use your IP address to limit repeated signup attempts and block spam. It's stored in a counter that is automatically deleted after one hour and isn't saved with your signup. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs for a short time for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, including refunds and cancellations, and to email you about it (for example your estimated ship date or a delay).
  • To email you about Whisper Wheels launches and related updates, if you joined the list.
  • To understand where interest comes from, such as which social posts, ads, regions and devices lead to signups and orders, so we can decide where to advertise.
  • To keep the signup form working and protect it from spam and abuse.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it with others for their own marketing. It's processed by the services that run the site for us:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website and provides approximate location from your connection.
  • Whop, which runs checkout, processes payments and sends your receipt.
  • Upstash, which stores the email list and a copy of order records.
  • Shipping carriers and fulfillment partners, who receive your name and address to deliver your order.
  • Our email provider, if we email you.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill and support your order and to meet tax and accounting requirements. We keep email-list signups until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Delete or see your data: email contact@whisperwheels.shop from the address you used, and we'll delete your information or send you a copy of what we hold (we may need to keep order records required by law).
  • Unsubscribe: use the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or email us.
  • On your device: clear this site's data in your browser to remove what's stored there.

Depending on where you live, such as California, you may have additional rights under local law. Contact us and we'll help.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
