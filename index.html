Why Whisper Wheels

Installation

How do I install it?

Slip one rubber cover over each wheel. That's the whole install. For the lid, wipe the rim clean, then peel the backing off the foam strip and press it along the rim where the lid closes.

Do I need any tools?

No. The kit is designed to be fitted by hand.

Can I take it off later?

The wheel covers are designed to slip off by hand. The foam strip is held on with adhesive, so removing it may leave some residue depending on your bin's surface.
