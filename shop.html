How it works

One simple step.

No tools, nothing to drill, unscrew or replace.

1

Slip on the covers

Tip the bin back and stretch one rubber cover over each wheel until it sits snugly around the tread. That's it.

Bonus for the lid: peel and press the included foam strip along the rim to silence lid noise.

Before you preorder: compatibility

Whisper Wheels is designed for common two-wheeled residential roll-out trash bins. Wheel size, width and shape vary between manufacturers, and we haven't yet published a list of bins it's confirmed to fit. Fit is not guaranteed.

If it doesn't fit your bin, contact us within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Many US bins are owned by a city or waste hauler. If yours is, check your local rules before adding anything to it.

Preorder FAQ

How preordering works.

When will I be charged?

Your card is charged when you place your preorder. That reserves your kit from our first production run.

When will it ship?

Ships with our first production run. We'll email your estimated ship date once production is scheduled. We don't guarantee a ship date: your kit ships as soon as the first run is ready, and you can request a full refund anytime before then.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Email contact@whisperwheels.shop anytime before your kit ships and we'll refund you in full. Refunds are issued when you ask; your preorder stays active until you do. If production runs late, we'll email you a new estimated date and you can choose to wait or cancel.

Where do you ship?

Within the United States only for now. See shipping & returns.

How much quieter will my bin be?

We haven't published measurements yet, so we're not making any numerical claims. The covers and strip are designed to help reduce wheel and lid noise.
