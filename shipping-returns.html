Shipping & Returns

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: preorders ship with our first production run, we email you your ship date, and you can cancel for a full refund anytime before your kit ships. If it doesn't fit your bin, tell us within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Preorders

Whisper Wheels is currently sold as a preorder. You pay when you place your order, which reserves a kit from our first production run. We'll email you an estimated ship date once production is scheduled, and again when your order ships.

If we can't ship by the date we've given you, we'll email you before that date with a new estimate. You can then choose to keep waiting or cancel for a full refund. If you don't reply, we'll keep your order active unless the law in your state requires otherwise.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only. Shipping within the US is free.

Cancelling a preorder

Email contact@whisperwheels.shop with the email address you ordered with any time before your kit ships. We'll refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

If it doesn't fit

We haven't yet published a list of bins Whisper Wheels is confirmed to fit. If the kit doesn't fit your bin, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order email and a photo of your bin's wheel. We'll refund you in full; you don't need to send the kit back unless we ask.

Returns after delivery

Unused kits in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Email us first and we'll send instructions. Once a foam strip has been applied it can't be reused, but we'll still refund you if the wheel covers didn't fit.

Damaged or missing items

If anything arrives damaged or is missing, email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll send a replacement or refund you.

Contact

Questions about an order: contact@whisperwheels.shop. Please include the email address you used at checkout.
