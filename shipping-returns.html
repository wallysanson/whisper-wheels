Shipping & Returns

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: preorders ship with our first production run and we email you your ship date. You can request a full refund anytime before your kit ships; refunds are issued on request, not automatically. If it doesn't fit your bin, tell us within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Preorders

Whisper Wheels is currently sold as a preorder. You pay when you place your order, which reserves a kit from our first production run. We'll email you an estimated ship date once production is scheduled, and again when your order ships. Ship dates are estimates and can change.

No guaranteed ship date

We don't promise a specific ship date for preorders. Your kit ships as soon as the first production run is ready, and we'll keep you updated by email along the way. Any date we share is our best estimate, not a guarantee. If you'd rather not wait, you can request a full refund anytime before your kit ships.

Refunds are on request

You can request a full refund at any time before your kit ships. Preorders stay active until you ask to cancel: we don't cancel or refund them automatically, including if production takes longer than expected. This lets customers who are happy to wait keep their place in the first run.

If your order is delayed

If we can't ship by the date we've given you, we'll email you before that date with a revised estimate and the option to cancel for a full refund. If you'd rather wait, you don't need to do anything. Where the law requires us to refund or cancel without a request, for example if a delay is long enough that your consent is required, we will follow it.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only. Shipping within the US is free.

How to request a refund

Email contact@whisperwheels.shop from, or mentioning, the email address you ordered with, any time before your kit ships. We'll refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

If it doesn't fit

We haven't yet published a list of bins Whisper Wheels is confirmed to fit. If the kit doesn't fit your bin, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order email and a photo of your bin's wheel. We'll refund you in full; you don't need to send the kit back unless we ask.

Returns after delivery

Unused kits in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Email us first and we'll send instructions. Once a foam strip has been applied it can't be reused, but we'll still refund you if the wheel covers didn't fit.

Damaged or missing items

If anything arrives damaged or is missing, email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll send a replacement or refund you.

Contact

Questions about an order: contact@whisperwheels.shop. Please include the email address you used at checkout.
