Shipping & Returns

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version: preorders ship as soon as production is ready; there's no exact delivery date yet, and we'll email you updates along the way. You can request a full refund anytime before your kit ships; refunds are issued on request, not automatically. Not happy with it for any reason? Return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Preorders

Whisper Wheels is currently sold as a preorder. Your kit is in production, and we don't have an exact delivery date yet. We'll keep you updated by email and let you know the moment your order ships. Full details are in our Preorder Policy.

Refunds are on request

You can request a full refund at any time before your kit ships. Preorders stay active until you ask to cancel: we don't cancel or refund them automatically, including if production takes longer than expected. This lets customers who are happy to wait keep their place in the first run.

If your order is delayed

If we can't ship by the date we've given you, we'll email you before that date with a revised estimate and the option to cancel for a full refund. If you'd rather wait, you don't need to do anything. Where the law requires us to refund or cancel without a request, for example if a delay is long enough that your consent is required, we will follow it.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only. Shipping within the US is free.

How to request a refund

Email contact@whisperwheels.shop from, or mentioning, the email address you ordered with, any time before your kit ships. We'll refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

30-day satisfaction guarantee

If you're not happy with Whisper Wheels for any reason, including if the kit doesn't fit your bin, you can return it for a full refund of the product price. Here's how it works:

  1. Email contact@whisperwheels.shop within 30 days of delivery with your order email and a quick note on what went wrong. We'll reply with return instructions.
  2. Ship the kit back within 14 days of receiving those instructions. It can be in any condition, and it's fine if the foam strip has been used.
  3. Once your return arrives, we'll refund the full product price to your original payment method.

Return shipping is paid by the customer. If your kit arrived damaged or defective, see below and we'll cover it.

Damaged or missing items

If anything arrives damaged or is missing, email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll send a replacement or refund you.

Contact

Questions about an order: contact@whisperwheels.shop. Please include the email address you used at checkout.
